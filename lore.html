FERRISVILLE

A town where sanity goes to die

Location

Ferrisville is a small, crumbling town on Yol Island, part of the country of Phantomgenesisland. Architecture is sparse. Weirdness is constant. There has never been a quiet day in Ferrisville. The town is considered one of the top "most fucked-up" places in the region.

Key Figures

Mild — A young resident who somehow ends up witnessing everything. He tries to mind his own business, but Ferrisville drags him into its chaos anyway.
Willy and Rick — Two hobos living in a doghouse. Willy is greedy, loud, and eventually loses his mind. Rick is the quieter one who counts money and, after certain events, becomes a streamer.
Sprunki ("The Green Whore") — A strange green creature with toxic bodily fluids. Found wounded by Willy. Her existence (and what happens to her) sets everything in motion.
Sprunki Jr. — Born from violence. His scream can knock out an entire town. Short existence. Crispy ending.
Garrett Reeves — A forest hunter. Always thinking about meat. Has two deer in his shed. They'll breed more. Then axe. Then meat.
Oystein Gustafsson — Lumberjack, Garrett's cousin. Tries to chop trees in peace but keeps getting dragged into nonsense.
Victor Novak — Former cop, now a hospital guard. The one who tries (and fails) to restore order.
Yakubovich — A guitarist. Mild's only friend. Short temper, quick fists, loyal.

Notable Events

- The Garage Brothel — A short-lived but legendary "business" run by Willy and Rick using Sprunki as "merchandise".
- The Sprunki Toxin — Causes swelling, hallucinations, and eventually death through the genitals. Willy found out the hard way.
- The Psych Ward Lockdown — Willy locked himself in after going completely insane. Died from a combination of toxins and overconsumption of his own supply.
- Rick's New Life — After Willy's death, Rick bought an apartment and a gaming PC with the brothel money. Became a streamer. Zero viewers.

Current State

Ferrisville continues to exist. The garage is abandoned. The Sprunki population is unknown. Rick streams to an empty chat. Mild is probably watching from somewhere, wondering what the fuck this town actually is.
